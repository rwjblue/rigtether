# Track M2: Rev A open-hardware reference design

## Outcome

Create, assemble, validate, and publish the first complete open-hardware RigTether reference design and Elecraft harness based on M1 evidence.

## Dependency order

This milestone is blocked on real M1 KX2/KX3 validation. Its detailed child issues should be created only after component, connector, mechanical, manufacturing, and validation constraints are known.

## Decomposition boundary

Do not pre-create detailed Rev A implementation issues until M1 validation records the actual architecture, parts constraints, measurements, and remaining risks.

## Exit criteria

- [ ] Production-oriented component choices are recorded in ADRs.
- [ ] Editable KiCad schematic and PCB source satisfy accepted safety requirements.
- [ ] The detachable KX2/KX3 harness and enclosure have source, drawings, and strain relief.
- [ ] At least one Rev A prototype is assembled and checked against a published bring-up plan.
- [ ] Human validation covers audio, CAT, PTT, faults, RF susceptibility, and thermal behavior.
- [ ] Complete source, BOM, fabrication, assembly, firmware, and known-limitations packages are published under the mapped licenses.
