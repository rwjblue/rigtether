# Code of Conduct

RigTether participants are expected to communicate professionally, discuss technical
claims with evidence, and make room for differing experience levels.

Harassment, personal attacks, deliberate safety misinformation, and disclosure of
private information are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove
contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the project and its
participants.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner rather than escalating them
in a public issue.
